Process

Process

This is genuinely a sequence, so we number it. Every project runs through all six stages — the only variable is how long design and build take for your scope, and how many time zones are involved.

01

Discovery

Stakeholder interviews, audit of what exists, and a written problem statement both sides sign off on before scope is set.

2 weeks
02

Definition

Information architecture, content strategy, and a fixed-fee proposal for the build phase — no open-ended estimates.

1–2 weeks
03

Design

Concepting through to high-fidelity, reviewed jointly with the engineering lead so nothing gets designed that can't be built on schedule.

3–8 weeks
04

Build

Front-end and platform work in parallel with content production, in two-week cycles with a working preview at the end of each.

3–12 weeks
05

Launch

Staged global rollout, monitoring, and a handover package: source files, documentation, and a recorded walkthrough for your team.

1 week
06

Support

Optional retainer for iteration, new features, or design capacity — month to month, no lock-in contract.

Ongoing

What you'll be asked for

Access to the people who'll use what we're building — not just the people who commissioned it. A single decision-maker who can sign off each stage within a week. And existing material: brand guidelines, analytics, support tickets, anything that shows how things actually work today.

What you'll get from us

A named design lead and build lead for the life of the project, a shared project log, working previews at the end of every two-week cycle, and full source-file ownership at handover.
