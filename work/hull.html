Replacing forty spreadsheets with one fleet operations system

A custom operations platform for scheduling, maintenance, and client billing across a 140-vessel global charter fleet.

The problem

Hull Maritime ran a 140-vessel global charter fleet on forty-plus spreadsheets — scheduling, maintenance logs, and client invoicing lived in separate files that never reconciled cleanly across offices.

The approach

We designed and built a single operations platform: a scheduling calendar, a maintenance log with automatic service alerts, and billing tied directly to charter records, translated for every regional office. The team trained on it in one afternoon.

40+spreadsheets replaced by one system
140vessels scheduled from a single calendar
4hrssaved per week, per fleet manager

"We didn't think a studio this size could build something this specific to us. They spent two weeks on our docks in three countries before writing a line of code."

Callum Reyes, Global Operations Director, Hull Maritime
